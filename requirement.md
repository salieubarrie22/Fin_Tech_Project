Requirement for Fin_Tech_Project
Functional Requirements
FR1: Users can sign up and log in securely.
FR2: Users can sen money to other users .
FR3: Users can recieve money.
FR4: Users can view transaction history.
FR5: Admin can manage users overall system reports.

Non-Functional Requirements
NFR1: The system shall ensure all data is encrypted during transfer.
NFR2: The app should load within 3 seconds on a 3G connection.
NFR3: The app must be available 99% of the time during business hours
